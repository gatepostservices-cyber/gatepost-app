# Gatepost Full Rate Book
INTERNAL. Owner and employees only. Do not send to customers.
Effective 15 Oct 2026. Rio Rancho / Albuquerque metro.
Prices are labor + standard supplies. Materials, dump fees, and GRT are extra unless noted.
Low-market aim: competitive with local one-crew operators, below big-franchise rates.

## How to use
- Quote the fixed price for the checked scope. Do not quote hourly on a client form.
- Handyman base rate: $65/hr. Minimum visit $75.
- Tax the job amount after any discount. GRT is its own line.
- Rio Rancho / Sandoval GRT 7.4375%. Albuquerque / Bernalillo GRT 7.625%.
- Locked aims stay locked: garden $625–$750; short fence cut-down ~$275; dirt ~2 CY $175–$250; junk full load aim $425.

## Rules
- Never invent a licensed-trade price. No electrical panels, new circuits, gas lines, or structural work.
- Stop if the job needs a license, a permit, or tile/wall demo you did not price.
- Materials at cost with approval, or customer supplies.
- Dump fees pass through. Do not write banned dump language.
- Discount before tax. One TOTAL. GRT is its own line.
- Quote valid 30 days. Pay when done. Cards may add 3%.
